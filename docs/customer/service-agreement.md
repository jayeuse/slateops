## 2. What this agreement covers

The Provider sells services. The Provider does not sell the software.

1. **Setup.** The Provider installs Slate Operations on one Windows 11 PC of the Customer, in the Customer's office. The Provider creates the first administrator account and checks that the Customer can sign in from one PC and one phone.
2. **Yearly support.** The Provider answers questions, helps with problems, applies published updates, and helps with backups and restores. The [support policy](support-policy.md) gives the details.

## 3. The software and its license

1. Slate Operations is licensed under the Elastic License 2.0. The license text is in the `LICENSE` file that comes with the software.
2. The license is free. The Customer gets the license from the licensor, not from this agreement. The Customer pays nothing for the license.
3. The license lets the Customer use, copy and distribute the software. It does not let anyone offer the software to others as a hosted or managed service. It does not let anyone go around a license key.
4. The software includes parts made by other people. Their own licenses apply to those parts.

## 4. What the current build does

1. The Customer gets an early build. An early build is a pre-release test build, and its setup program is not code-signed. It has every feature and no edition limits. It checks no license file. Setup and the app may name the edition "Desk". No Desk limits apply.
2. An early build gets no automatic update prompt. The app shows an update only when a release is published as a stable release. Until then, the Provider installs each update by hand as part of support.
3. Edition terms, edition prices and license files start with the first licensed version. That version needs a new written agreement or an amendment. This agreement does not promise any edition, price or license file.
4. The Provider can change the software between builds. The Provider tells the Customer before an update that removes or changes a feature the Customer uses.

## 5. Fees and payment

1. Setup fee: [setup fee] [currency], paid once, within [payment days] days after setup.
2. Yearly support fee: [yearly support fee] [currency] for each support year, paid within [payment days] days after the start of that year.
3. The Provider gives an official receipt for each payment.
4. Fees do not include the Customer's PC, Windows, internet service, email account, backup drive or other hardware.

## 6. Term and renewal

1. The first support year starts on [start date] and lasts 12 months.
2. Support renews for another 12 months only when both parties agree in writing and the Customer pays the yearly support fee.
3. When support ends, the Customer keeps the software, the license and all data. The software keeps working without support.

## 7. Termination

1. Either party can end this agreement with [notice days] days' written notice.
2. Either party can end it at once if the other party breaks a main term and does not fix it within [notice days] days after written notice.
3. If the Customer ends the agreement without cause, paid fees are not returned. If the Provider ends it without cause, the Provider returns the unused part of the yearly support fee.
4. At the end, the Provider gives back or deletes any copy of the Customer's data that the Provider holds.

## 8. What support does not cover

1. Hardware faults, Windows faults, and problems with the Customer's network, internet service or email provider.
2. Data loss when the Customer did not keep the backup drive connected, or ignored a failed-backup warning.
3. Changes that the Customer or another person made to the software, its files or its Linux part.
4. Other PCs or other installations than the one that the Provider set up.
5. Training beyond the [visits per year] visits that the support policy includes.
6. New features, custom development and data import from other systems, unless both parties agree in writing.

## 9. Liability

1. The Provider does the setup and support with reasonable skill and care.
2. The software comes "as is" under the Elastic License 2.0. The Provider gives no promise that the software has no faults.
3. The Provider's total liability under this agreement is not more than [liability cap amount] [currency].
4. Neither party is liable for lost profits, lost business or indirect loss.
5. These limits do not apply where the law does not allow a limit.

## 10. Data and confidentiality

1. The Customer's data stays on the Customer's PC. The [data and privacy statement](data-and-privacy.md) explains how.
2. The Provider accesses the Customer's PC or data only when the Customer asks for it, and only for the support task.
3. Each party keeps the other party's confidential information private.
